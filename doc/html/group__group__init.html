void init_data ( )

This method is a wrapper, and is used to call extractMDEdataForVoffset without any arguments passed from the main.cpp scope, to avoid 'out of scope' issues. It initialise and declare 2 local integer variables: level and index, that are arguments in extractMDEdataForVoffset. All other arguments passed to extractMDEdataForVoffset are in the utils.cpp global scope.
